About Our School

A learning community committed to academic growth, discipline, talents and responsible citizenship.

DR SHEIN SECONDARY SCHOOL

DR SHEIN SECONDARY SCHOOL is located at Ziwa Kije, Micheweni District, North Pemba, Zanzibar, Tanzania.

The school provides an environment where students can develop academic knowledge, practical skills, discipline, creativity and confidence.

We believe education should prepare students not only for examinations, but also for responsible and productive life in society.

  • Quality teaching and learning
  • Discipline and good character
  • Talent and creativity development
  • Student participation and leadership
  • Positive relationship with parents and community
